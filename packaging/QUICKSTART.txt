================================================================
  PCL Designer — Quick Start
================================================================

This folder contains:

  - The PCL Designer executable for your platform
  - USER_GUIDE.md   (full instructions; open in any browser or
                     markdown viewer for nicer formatting)
  - QUICKSTART.txt  (this file)

----------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO LAUNCH (pick your platform)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  macOS (Apple Silicon: M1, M2, M3, M4):

      Open Terminal.app and run these three lines:

          cd "/path/to/this/folder"
          chmod +x PCLDesigner-macos-arm64
          xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine PCLDesigner-macos-arm64
          ./PCLDesigner-macos-arm64

      Or double-click PCLDesigner-macos-arm64 in Finder. macOS will
      say "developer cannot be verified" — click OK, then right-click
      the file → Open → Open. macOS remembers after the first time.

  Windows (64-bit):

      Double-click PCLDesigner-windows-x86_64.exe in File Explorer.

      Windows SmartScreen will warn:
        "Windows protected your PC."
      Click the small blue "More info" link at the top of the popup,
      then click the "Run anyway" button that appears.

  Linux (64-bit):

      Open a terminal in this folder and run:

          chmod +x PCLDesigner-linux-x86_64
          ./PCLDesigner-linux-x86_64

----------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
----------------------------------------------------------------

A black console window opens with a banner that ends:

    Open in your browser: http://localhost:8765
    Close this window (or Ctrl+C) to quit.

Your default web browser opens automatically to that address with
the PCL Designer form. Fill in your design parameters, click
"Generate optimal design," and the optimal design matrix appears
below the form with CSV and JSON download buttons.

To quit: close the console window, or press Ctrl+C inside it.
